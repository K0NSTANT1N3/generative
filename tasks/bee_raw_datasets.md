# Raw and regulatory bee-toxicity data catalog

Checked 26 October 2026. This is a bounded search of publicly discoverable resources, not a claim of worldwide completeness. **C** = confirmed in a primary page/API/paper; **D** = counted in an actually downloaded artifact; **NR** = not recovered. File sizes below are exact bytes where an integer is given. Access to public files was checked without credentials. Dataset metadata, downloaded small files, and worksheet/count evidence are retained under `/tmp/bee-raw-audit/`.

## Interpretation that matters for dataset selection

These are not mutually disjoint datasets. ECOTOX→ApisTox, ECOTOX→harmonized ED10, ECOTOX→Kniss and OPP DER→Farruggia are explicit provenance paths. PPDB contributes to ApisTox, Lewis and the Tosi aggregates. The Tosi mixture/sublethal tables also repeat compounds and may cite studies already abstracted into ECOTOX. Deduplicate at structure, study/reference/MRID, species, route, duration and endpoint level before describing the result as additional independent evidence.

Dose in µg/bee, concentration in µg/mL, dietary concentration in mg/kg diet, surface dose and daily dose in µg/bee/day are different targets. Likewise LD50, NOAEL, LOAEL, ED10, survival counts and gene expression are different outcomes. Preserve `<`/`>` qualifiers; never train an ordinary exact regression by silently replacing censored values with the bound. Extrapolated/harmonized ED10 values should be identifiable separately from directly measured endpoints.

For a SMILES project, chemical names and CAS identifiers are resolvable starting points, not verified structures. Commercial formulations, mixtures, microbes, oils/extracts and unresolved code names must remain separately flagged. Compare molecular generalization using structure/scaffold splits; thousands of replicate/timepoint rows for two chemicals do not form a thousand-molecule training set.
