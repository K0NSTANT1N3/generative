# Bee-toxicity dataset audit: model corpora, derived sets, and primary data

Scope: public chemical and molecular bee-toxicity data that could supply labels for bee-safe molecular generation. Counts are not interchangeable: a measurement row, a chemical record, a unique standardized structure, and a task-labelled structure are different units. “Open download” means the asset appears directly accessible without an application; it does **not** imply an open license. I did not infer licenses from free viewing. This is a discoverable-source inventory, not a claim that every scattered assay in literature has been found.

## Key lineage / overlap cautions

1. The apparent “many benchmark datasets” collapse into a few lineages: original BeeTOX/Wang (GACNN; then PE-GCL and likely derivatives), admetSAR/ECOTOX-derived 195-example set (toxCSM), and BeeToxAI’s contact/oral curation (plus Yang et al. graph-kernel mirror). ApisTox reuses primary ECOTOX/PPDB/BPDB records; the 2026 oral set and BeeEcoTox also cite overlapping sources. Without structure+CAS+measurement-level joins, avoid summing them as independent examples.
2. Thresholds differ materially: BeeToxAI/ApisTox classify LD50 ≤11 μg/bee as toxic; CropCSM, Deep-PK/old ADMET-SAR and 2026 oral SAR commonly use a 100 μg/bee cutoff with potentially different “high/low” label semantics. Those are different prediction tasks.
3. “BeeToxAI dataset size” is not one number. 2,543 is the paper’s initial compiled compound count, 734 is ApisTox’s counted raw BeeToxAI molecular set, 472 is unique cleaned structures, 403 are reported with labels, while contact/oral task datasets have their own smaller sample counts.
4. “Available online” is not “openly licensed.” Several official sources have direct files but no explicit dataset license; several article licenses do not automatically cover upstream ECOTOX/PPDB/OCHEM material.

## Negative searches / naming corrections

- I found no BeeTox-named dataset in the TDC public benchmark catalog/repository search; TDC code licenses do not grant licenses to every imported dataset. Deep-PK’s `bee_tox` is a separate 182-record task and should not be called “TDC BeeTox.”
- ADMET-SAR does have a honeybee toxicity model/data lineage: official admetSAR 2 docs list the 195-example ECOTOX-derived corpus, and admetSAR 3.0 lists a separate model training total of 620. The prediction endpoint/model-statistics page is not itself a row-level open dataset release.
- “BeeSafe 1” as a distinct public benchmark was not found. BeeSafe 2.0’s repository does expose two bee toxicity XLSX files, but no license and no verified row count were available in the checked metadata.

## What remains unverified

- Exact row and structure overlap among ApisTox, BeeTOX, BeeToxAI, CropCSM, BeeSafe 2.0, BeeEcoTox, PE-GCL, admetSAR 3, Deep-PK, and the new 2026 oral set. A reproducible crosswalk requires downloading files and matching canonical structures plus CAS and assay metadata; this note does not claim it was computed.
- Dataset-file licenses, sizes, formats and exact row counts for BeeSafe 2.0, BeeEcoTox underlying Figshare files, admetSAR 3.0 training cohort, PE-GCL, and portions of CropCSM. A public model/service page alone is not evidence that training data are released.
- The unit conflict in the wild-bee record count, and current live file availability at the legacy Beetox server.
- Whether 2026 Mendeley chronic ED10 inputs are all in the companion 2025/2026 harmonized Zenodo workbook or include other sources; confirm by comparing their downloadable manifests/content.
