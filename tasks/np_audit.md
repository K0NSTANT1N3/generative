# Microbial natural-product generation audit (NP-focused subtask)

Scope: published computational projects from 2022–2026 that actually generate small-molecule natural-product or natural-product-inspired structures. Antibiotic-first de novo projects (Cell 2025 and JACS Au 2025) are being audited separately by the parent task's antibiotic-compute agent. I include those by name only in the scope note below, to avoid duplicate rows. “Not reported” means I could not confirm the quantity in the paper, supplement, or official project repository. Dataset sizes and current download sizes below are primary-source values where marked confirmed; an estimate is explicitly labelled.

### Exclusions / boundary cases

- **Cell 2025, “A generative deep learning approach to de novo antibiotic design”** (Krishnan et al., DOI [10.1016/j.cell.2025.07.033]) and **JACS Au 2025, “Generative Deep Learning Pipeline Yields Potent Gram-Negative Antibiotics”** (DOI [10.1021/jacsau.5c00602]) are core direct antibiotic-generation papers, but are assigned to the separate antibiotic-compute audit to avoid duplicate research. The Cell paper uses CReM plus VAE-based compound design guided by antibacterial prediction/fragment conditions; JACS Au uses a chemical language model trained on ChEMBL, MEGx natural products, then antibiotic transfer learning.
- **NP-VAE** is included despite being a general complex-molecule model because the paper explicitly targets natural compounds, reports natural-product generation and has 12M parameters.
- A 2026 small-molecule natural-products foundation-model paper (Nature Machine Intelligence, DOI [10.1038/s42256-026-01226-8]) was found, but the available abstract/data description concerns pretraining and downstream classification/regression, not a reported molecule-generation model; I therefore do not count it as a generative project.
- **NPAtlas and COCONUT are datasets, not generators.** NPAtlas is particularly useful as a microbial-only held-out reference set, but not as a generator training corpus if one requires unrestricted commercial reuse: current release is CC BY-NC 4.0.

## What the domain already covers and what appears open

Published work already demonstrates unconditional NP-like molecular language modeling (Tay et al.; NPGPT), motif-based NP-inspired conditional/scaffold generation (NIMO), and generation around a natural-product seed using a VAE (NP-VAE; Porphyra-334 ChemVAE case study). Antibiotic-specific molecule generation is now also represented by the Cell 2025 and JACS Au 2025 papers handled in the companion audit. Thus the broad claim “nobody has generated natural-product-like molecules” is false.

The narrower gap is still compelling: I found no 2022–2026 paper that trains an openly reproducible, microbial-only natural-product generative model and conditions its generation on measured antibacterial activity plus Gram-negative uptake, resistance avoidance, and/or mechanistic targets, then validates novelty against the full microbial NP literature/database and tests generated molecules experimentally. Existing NP-trained models mostly reproduce the broad NP distribution, optimize generic properties, or use predicted bioactivity; NPGPT's bioactivity illustration is EGFR docking, not antibiotics. The exact gap should be framed around a rigorously defined biological objective and leakage-aware novelty, rather than generic “NP generation.”

## Validity and fast filtering tools

- **RDKit** ([project](https://www.rdkit.org/), [documentation](https://www.rdkit.org/docs/)): parse SMILES/SDF, sanitize valence/aromaticity/rings, canonicalize, identify duplicates and compute fingerprints/descriptors. For practical generated-small-molecule validation this is the obvious free local checker. The model papers use it, but do not report per-sample timing; **runtime is not reported in these primary sources**. A local benchmark should report median/p95 across representative molecule sizes; RDKit parse/sanitize is normally lightweight, but I do not assert a measured runtime here.
- For natural-product plausibility filters, RDKit's bundled **NP_Score** and the open **NPClassifier** can add NP-likeness and superclass/class/pathway predictions; these are plausibility/classification signals, not chemical validity checks or experimental bioactivity. [NPClassifier code/data](https://github.com/mwang87/NP-Classifier).
